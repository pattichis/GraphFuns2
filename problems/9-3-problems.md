# Lesson 9-3: Writing Exponential Functions
Construct exponential functions $y = a b ^x$:<br>
* given two points.
* given a graph.
* given a description.

For exponential decay, we have:
$\qquad y = a (1-r)^t$.<br>
where:<br>
$\qquad y$ &nbsp; is the final amount.<br>
$\qquad a$ &nbsp; is the initial amount.<br>
$\qquad t$ &nbsp; represents time.<br>
$\qquad r$ &nbsp; represents the rate of decay expressed as a decimal: &nbsp; $0< r < 1$.<br>
